## The business
Lakeview Fabrication is an independently owned, 75-person fabrication shop in Janesville, Wisconsin. Founded in 1998, it serves customers across Wisconsin, Illinois, Iowa and Minnesota from one 48,000-square-foot facility. Work includes structural steel assemblies, custom fabrication and repeat production welding to customer drawings. The names and location describe a fictional business, not an actual company with a similar name.

The shop grew from 60 to 75 employees when 15 welders joined on July 13. Today there are 45 welders, 16 people in fit-up, machining and shipping, six in quality and engineering, four in sales and customer service, and four in leadership and administration. It runs day and evening shifts. Supervisor and planning roles sit within the relevant operating groups.

Structural work anchors revenue but competes for capital and offers lower margins. # Controlled work and release procedures

Fictional workshop data. All companies, people, communications and figures are invented.

These are fictional workflow procedures for the exercise. They are not welding procedure specifications, technical acceptance criteria or instructions for performing a weld. Participants should design document checks and escalation practice using these facts; actual settings and acceptance limits require approved job documents and qualified technical review.

## PR01 | Job-start document check | Revision 4 | Effective July 13, 2026
Owner: Quality. Before starting a new or changed job, match the traveler job number and revision to the controlled drawing. Verify that the fixture identifier matches the traveler. If the revision or fixture differs, stop the job-start process and ask the shift supervisor and Quality to resolve it. A paper binder cannot overrule the controlled document register. Record the version used and the verifier on the traveler.

## PR02 | First-piece release | Revision 3 | Effective July 13, 2026
Owner: Operations and Quality. A new setup or changed drawing requires a first-piece check before the remaining batch proceeds. The designated qualified verifier records the required job-specific checks and release on the traveler. If that person is unavailable, escalate to the supervisor; elapsed time and prior operator experience do not substitute for release. The actual inspection criteria come from the approved drawing and applicable job requirements.

## PR03 | Defect containment | Revision 2 | Effective May 4, 2026
Owner: Quality. Identify the affected lot, segregate suspect units, preserve the traveler and record the observed condition. Notify the supervisor and Quality. Quality determines disposition and any broader containment. A corrected part does not close a customer claim. Customer acceptance and documented corrective-action verification are separate steps.

## PR04 | Observed job signoff | Revision 2 | Effective July 13, 2026
Owner: Operations. Orientation attendance records exposure to content. Before independent assignment to a changed job, the supervisor observes the employee locate the current drawing, reconcile the traveler and fixture, explain the first-piece release, and escalate a mismatch. Record the date, job family and observer. Completion is not an unrestricted welding qualification.

## PR05 | Commercial release | Revision 5 | Effective September 1, 2026
Owner: GM. Quotes use approved service scope and the applicable planning margin floor; exceptions need GM approval. Planner confirmation is needed for capacity and ship commitments. Finance clears credit holds or writes an exception. A pilot budget does not authorize repeat production. Sales owns coordination, not automatic release authority.
