# Lakeview brand guide

Fictional workshop data. All companies, people, communications and figures are invented.
Scenario clock: September 8, 2026, 8:00 a.m. Central.

## Identity
Lakeview Fabrication. Descriptor: Structural steel. Custom fabrication. Production welding.
Position: A practical fabrication partner for equipment builders and contractors who need the drawing, the part and the handoff to agree.
Voice: plainspoken, specific and accountable. Use concrete parts, buyer needs and next steps. Explain the approval sequence. Avoid unsupported superlatives, guarantees and invented customer endorsements.

## Visual system
Lake Blue #56628E is the primary brand color. Foundry Gold #E29D51 is the accent. Charcoal #4D4D4D carries body text. Warm Gray #E2DFDA is the supporting background. White #FFFFFF is used for clean pages and reverse text on blue. Use charcoal text on gold. Gold is an accent, not small text on a light background. This palette follows the organizer's supplied reference.
Use Arial Bold for headlines and Arial for body copy. Keep headlines short. Use large margins, a clear left edge and thin rules. Put numbers beside their definitions. Use flat fills and simple structural geometry. Use blue headings, charcoal body text, and gold rules or selected highlights.

## Supplied assets
lakeview-logo.svg is the primary editable vector logo. lakeview-logo-reverse.svg is for dark backgrounds. lakeview-mark.svg is the compact mark. The mark uses joined frame members and a horizon bar. Clear space around the logo equals the height of the L mark's horizontal member. Minimum width: 180 pixels digital or 1.5 inches print. Do not stretch, rotate or add effects. A monochrome blue, charcoal or white version is acceptable.
brand-board.pdf shows the palette and applications. campaign-layout.svg is an editable 1600 by 900 slide/campaign canvas. This starter canvas contains neutral brand text, not a completed campaign answer. No photography is required for the workshop.

## Claims and approval
Approved: 75-person Midwest shop; three listed service families; customer-drawing review; first-article documentation; repeat releases after acceptance. Quote the applicable date for a performance statistic and calculate it from the supplied data. Do not claim sanitary capability, guaranteed lead times, universal certification, a perfect quality record or customer approval of marketing testimonials. Website copy in the packet is historical source material to review, not automatic approval to repeat it.

## Example voice
Use: "Send your frame drawing and expected release volumes. We will review scope, first-article requirements and a feasible start date."
Avoid: "Flawless quality. Any metal. Any deadline."
