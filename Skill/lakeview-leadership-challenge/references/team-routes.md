# Team routes

Paths are relative to the repository root. Read only the assigned route and sources. The option examples are starting points, not answer keys.

## Marketing
Read 01-Marketing/BRIEF.md and EVIDENCE.md in that folder.
- Investigate: growth segment, channel results, buyer experience, or credibility of claims.
- Diagnose: is the problem audience fit, follow-up, capability, buyer requirements, or proof? Check the economics, buyer interviews, channel history and current limits.
- Decide: a focused segment test, a channel change, or a buyer-proof package. Keep the $18,000 six-week budget separate from the $19,000 historical Q2 spend. Do not turn spend into a fixed meeting forecast.
- Before commitment: What fact supports the choice? What other explanation will you test? What budget and seller time will you use? Who checks the claims and results, and when? What finding would make you change course?

## Operations
Read 02-Operations/BRIEF.md and EVIDENCE.md in that folder, including the OP06 scenario amendment.
- Investigate: missing tooling, preparation and handoff, shared-tool scheduling, or usable capacity.
- Diagnose: is the delay caused by missing tools, two teams needing the tool at once, wrong schedule blocks, or no time to return the tool? The current trial combines several fixes, so it cannot prove which one worked.
- Decide: buy duplicate fixtures, improve the shared kit, change the schedule, or run a small test. Weigh the $100,000 cost of two $50,000 fixtures, recurring labor, schedule flexibility and space. Space and relocation costs still need estimates.
- Before commitment: How will you tell which cause matters? What cost or space tradeoff will you accept? Who checks the tool, the schedule and the downstream capacity? What number will you review, and what would make you change course?

## Training
Read 03-Training/BRIEF.md and EVIDENCE.md in that folder. Use the analysis phase of ADDIE in plain language.
- Investigate: are new welders ready, are work conditions getting in the way, or both?
- Diagnose: compare group, shift and work type. Check signoffs, current procedures, old training and supervisor coverage. Pick the people and behavior that need attention. New-hire rejects do not explain every problem on the established evening shift.
- Decide: targeted practice with observed signoff, fix the document/release process, a longer coaching plan, or a small fact-finding step. An AI coach is optional. People still make competency and first-piece release decisions.
- Before commitment: Who fixes the documents and release coverage? Which people and behaviors get a readiness check? Who provides coaching time and when? What result would make you adjust or escalate?

## Sales (optional fourth team)
Read 04-Sales/Sales-BRIEF.md and 04-Sales/Sales-EVIDENCE.md in that folder.
- Investigate: protect commitments, move workable opportunities forward, clear blockers, or custom. Choose a rule for accounts that compete.
- Diagnose: connect the six accounts and eight opportunities. Check contacts, owners, duplicate RFQs, quality, credit, scope and capacity. Decide which blocker matters most.
- Decide: a call-priority list, opportunity decision board, or custom workflow. Choose an owner, success measure and seller time. Do not contact customers or change the CRM in the exercise.
- Before commitment: Will you clean up the account and opportunity list first? Which quality, scope, credit, capacity or pilot condition controls the next action? Who keeps owners and dates current? What new fact changes the next action?
