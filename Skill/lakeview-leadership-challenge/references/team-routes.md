# Team routes

Paths are relative to Public. Read only the assigned route and sources. The option examples are hypotheses to explore, not answer keys.

## Marketing
Read 01-Marketing/BRIEF.md and EVIDENCE.md in that folder.
- Investigate: growth-segment opportunity, channel efficiency, buyer experience, or credibility of claims.
- Diagnose: audience fit versus follow-up for channel issues; capability and buyer requirements for segment issues; operational proof versus generic messaging for credibility issues. Check economics, buyer interviews, channel history and current constraints.
- Decide: a focused segment test, channel reallocation with diagnosis, or a buyer-proof/qualification intervention. Accept cash and seller-time costs. Keep the $18,000 six-week envelope distinct from $19,000 historical Q2 spending. Shared costs count once. No fixed meeting forecast from proportional spend. Other evidence-supported solutions remain valid.
- Decision checks to discuss before commitment: What audience or channel evidence supports the choice, and what rival explanation will you test? What cash and seller time will you commit, and who will confirm availability? Who validates customer-facing claims and reviews comparable qualified outcomes, how often, and what result changes the audience or follow-up approach? Do not require a particular campaign format.

## Operations
Read 02-Operations/BRIEF.md and EVIDENCE.md in that folder, including the OP06 scenario amendment.
- Investigate: tooling availability, preparation/handoff, shared-tool scheduling, or usable capacity.
- Diagnose: distinguish missing tools, simultaneous demand, incorrect blocks and missing turnaround allowances. The trial bundles dedicated availability, labeling and staging; it does not isolate their effects. Request missing release/transfer timestamps or floor measurements instead of inventing them.
- Decide: duplicate the fixtures, improve shared-kit preparation, reschedule shared use, or a justified combination/test. Accept capital, recurring labor, schedule flexibility and space consequences. Duplication is two $50,000 fixtures; available production space cannot accommodate both without relocation. Space dimensions and relocation cost require estimates, clearly labeled if proposed. No automatic best purchase or automatic scheduling winner.
- Decision checks to discuss before commitment: How will you separate availability, preparation and scheduling effects at both stations? Which capital/space, recurring labor or schedule cost do you accept, and what must be verified before commitment? Who preserves release checks and confirms ready work, operators, material and downstream capacity so saved setup time can become useful output? Choose a review measure and a trigger to change the trial.

## Training
Read 03-Training/BRIEF.md and EVIDENCE.md in that folder. Use the analysis phase of ADDIE in plain language.
- Investigate: employee capability/readiness, working conditions/support, both, or custom.
- Diagnose: compare cohort, shift and work type. Inspect signoffs, legacy training, controlled procedures and supervisor coverage. Choose the audience and behavior/barrier. Higher new-hire rejection rates do not explain all established-evening deterioration.
- Decide: targeted practice with observed signoff, document/release corrections, a longer coaching program, or further diagnosis with correction of known conflicts. A station AI coach is optional. Longer programs require staffing assumptions beyond the supplied 20-minute session and 10-minute check allowance. People retain competency and first-piece release decisions.
- Decision checks to discuss before commitment: Who corrects controlled-document distribution and qualified-release coverage? Which employees and behaviors will receive observed readiness checks, including verification of missing records and July changes? Who supplies recurring coaching/assessment time, how often, and what evidence triggers adjustment or escalation? A focused cohort is valid when justified; record the risk and owner or later review for deferred cohorts. Do not require an AI coach or a whole-workforce program.

## Sales (optional fourth team)
Read Sales-BRIEF.md and Sales-EVIDENCE.md at the Public root.
- Investigate: protect commitments, advance feasible opportunities, resolve blockers, or custom. Choose a principle when accounts compete.
- Diagnose: connect six accounts A001-A006 and opportunities O001-O008. Reconcile current contacts/owners, duplicate RFQs, conditional programs, quality, credit, scope and capacity. Decide which uncertainty or blocker governs priority.
- Decide: call-prioritization workflow, opportunity decision board, or custom. Choose an owner, success measure and accepted seller effort. No actual customer contact or CRM updates. Optional Sales-OPTION files provide supporting views only after the initial report/deck if requested; they do not replace the after-action sequence.
- Decision checks to discuss before commitment: Will you adopt the dated account/duplicate/contact/owner reconciliation before ranking? Which quality, scope, credit, capacity and pilot/acceptance conditions control the actions? Who maintains owners and deadlines, with what seller time and review cadence, and what new evidence changes the next action? Initial reconciliation and ongoing updates are separate responsibilities; make both choices explicit.
