# Lakeview artifact design

Keep Lakeview blue #56628E, gold #E29D51, charcoal #4D4D4D, warm gray #E2DFDA and white. Use white/warm gray as the foundation, charcoal for headlines and gold/blue selectively. Avoid full purple backgrounds as the default.

Use editorial composition: a strong image-led report cover, distinct evidence and recommendation layouts, generous margins, disciplined typography and concise source captions. Use Arial or an available close sans serif with clear headline/body contrast. Charts should explain the decision with direct labels and meaningful comparisons. Keep tables editable where supported. Icons should clarify actions or responsibilities rather than fill space.

## Non-negotiable finish
The report and slides must look like a leadership readout, not a text export. Use the matching area image from `Brand/` as a deliberate visual anchor. Include the Lakeview logo or mark, a blue eyebrow, a short charcoal headline, a gold rule or highlight, and clear page numbers or footer treatment. Use large numbers, short evidence cards and one visual comparison where the data supports it. Leave generous white space.

Do not use a generic Word-style page, a centered title over a thin photo strip, a long bullet wall, a dense default table, tiny gray source text, or a full purple/blue page as the main layout. Do not shrink type to fit. Keep body text at least 16 pt on slides and 10.5 pt in reports; keep source notes readable. A slide should communicate one idea in five seconds.

## Required artifact layouts
- **Findings report:** image-led cover; an evidence page with three to four cards or a chart; a “what we know / what we still do not know” page; a recommendation page with action, owner, budget/time and check-in; a short evidence note.
- **Two-slide deck:** Slide 1 is “What We Found” with one decisive headline, two or three large evidence numbers and one comparison or visual. Slide 2 is “What We Should Do” with the action, tradeoff, owner, success measure and AI’s role. Keep each slide to one message.

Render the files before delivery and inspect them at presentation size. Fix clipping, awkward wrapping, weak contrast, excessive text and unused space before handing them to the team.

Area images are in `Brand/`: lakeview-marketing.png, lakeview-operations.png, lakeview-training.png, lakeview-sales.png. Use the matching image with intentional cropping and preserve aspect ratio. They are AI-generated fictional editorial imagery, not evidence of an actual facility or event. Avoid small text over busy imagery. The elevated Marketing benchmark is a visual precedent, not participant evidence or an answer source.

For the Results slide, emphasize the main consequence, an understandable workshop score, one remaining issue and the next decision. Do not fill it with a fabricated forecast. Check rendered text, contrast and layout before delivery. Keep the original recommendation intact when adding Results.
