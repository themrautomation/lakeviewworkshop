# Training sequence

Training now follows the common [decision sequence](decision-sequence.md) and the Training section of [team routes](team-routes.md). Its three checkpoints cover explanation, audience/barrier, and intervention/success. Deliver the findings report and two-slide recommendation first. Wait for the participant's after-action request before applying [the prepared outcomes](after-action-outcomes.md), then deliver a fictional 90-day report and Results slide. This replaces the previous simulator workflow.
