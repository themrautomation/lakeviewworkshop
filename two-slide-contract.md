# Common two-slide contract

Use 16:9, Lakeview blue #56628E, gold #E29D51, charcoal #4D4D4D, warm gray #E2DFDA and white. Arial or a close available sans serif. The supplied template has exactly two editable slides. Keep both slide titles exactly as below, with the team's name as a small label. Use roughly 60–90 words per slide plus compact source references. Prefer a clear comparison or one important number over dense prose.

## Slide 1: What We Found
- A one-sentence statement of the single priority.
- Two or three evidence points, ideally one simple editable chart or table when helpful.
- Quantified significance in the supported units, with period and denominator.
- A concise reason leadership should care and any material uncertainty.
- Source IDs alongside the evidence or in a readable footer.

## Slide 2: What We Should Do
- The specific intervention and first practical step.
- Expected impact, with observed baseline separated from a target or scenario.
- What must change, the accountable role and any cost or dependency supported by the data.
- AI role: a defined execution task, support for a person, or no implementation role. Explain why this fits the mechanism of the problem.
- Source IDs and necessary assumptions.

The INITIAL deck has exactly two slides with no cover or weekly-monitoring section. The findings report is a separate deliverable. After the participant requests the 90-day after-action, append exactly one Results slide under the contract below. Use speaker notes for short calculation detail if supported, but keep assumptions that change the interpretation visible on the slide.


## Slide 3: Results (after-action stage only)
- Prominent label: Fictional 90-day after-action.
- One main consequence linked to the team's actual decision.
- Workshop outcome score, labeled /100 (not a percent improvement or probability).
- What improved, what remained unresolved, and the next leadership decision.
- Distinguish source baseline from authored scenario events. Do not invent financial or quality effects to populate the slide.

Deliver a new three-slide deck preserving the original two slides when the file is available. Otherwise deliver a standalone Results slide or complete paste-ready content with a candid limitation. Do not revise the original recommendation retroactively to match the ending.
